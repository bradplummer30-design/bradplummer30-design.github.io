Support — Mint Marble

Mint Marble is a portrait marble game for iPhone and iPad with 1,400 courses across 72 stages. It requires iOS 15 or later, plays offline and has no accounts. It is free to play, with optional one-time in-app purchases (marble finishes, landing effects and Remove Ads). Ads are optional: they are possible only for players aged 18 or older who allow them.

How to play

  • Roll: press behind the marble to roll forward; the marble moves away from your finger. Press in front of it to brake or roll backward, and beside it or diagonally to steer. Lift your finger to slow down and reposition.
  • Jump: stop in the centre of a glowing ring and lift to settle. The small bar under the course information fills while you hold still, and the jump starts by itself. Passing quickly across a ring does not launch you.
  • Watch the edge: after a fall, "Try from checkpoint" restarts from the latest gold checkpoint you touched, and "Try again" restarts from the start. Retries are free. Some courses have one checkpoint, and some expert runs have none.
  • Pause: tap the II button. Leaving the app, locking the screen or opening Control Center also pauses the run; tap "Keep playing" to carry on.

Stars and progress

  • Collect the gold stars, then finish the course to bank them. Quitting or failing never adds stars to your record. Stars you have banked stay yours when you replay a course.
  • Courses marked "3 stars required" need all three before the finish. Some jumps and slides wait until you have collected the stars before them.
  • Finishing a course opens the next one. Your Journey lets you browse every world from the start, but browsing never unlocks a course: a locked course says which course to finish first.
  • Surfaces behave differently. Bead beds dip and crunch, sand adds drag, mud grips, and on ice you should brake early. On blizzard courses, press into the gust and cross in the calm. Launcher rims turn amber before firing, cannons fire across the path, and swinging arches and pink blockers reward timing. Lanterns flicker on night courses.

Settings

  • Reduced motion stops the moving Home preview and calms decorative and celebration animation. Gameplay hazards and essential weather cues keep moving.
  • Steady lanterns keeps night-course lanterns lit, with no flicker or dark spells.
  • Sound effects can be switched off, and the level can be adjusted and previewed with "Listen".

The Collection and purchases

The Collection shows every marble finish and landing effect. The Original Chrome marble and the Mint Ripple landing are included. Opening an item or turning its preview never buys or equips it. Paid finishes are one-time purchases through Apple, at the price shown for your region. The six pastel landings are sold together as one set. "Buy" opens Apple's purchase sheet. A purchase that is waiting for approval, such as Ask to Buy, shows as pending until Apple completes it. After a purchase the item shows as owned, and "Use this finish" equips it. Finishes change only the look; every marble handles the same. Players who chose Under 13 or 13 to 17 at the age question, and players who have not answered it, see a grown-up check (a short multiplication question) before Buy, Restore Purchases and links that leave the app.

If something you bought is missing (on a new device, after a reinstall, or after a purchase that completed later), open Settings, then "Purchases", and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Restore brings back finishes and Remove Ads together. It never charges you again, and it does not restore progress. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; a refunded item is removed. A text backup never restores or proves a purchase.

Ads and Remove Ads

Ads are optional. Mint Marble asks which age range you are in (Under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older), and every answer gives you the whole game. Only players who choose "18 or older" are asked whether to allow ads. If you allow them, iOS then asks whether Mint Marble may allow tracking. Ads can appear only when you have chosen 18 or older, allowed ads and allowed tracking. In every other case there are no ads, nothing else changes, and nothing needs to be bought.

When ads are allowed, a short full-screen ad can appear occasionally after you finish a course and tap to continue from its results screen. It never appears during a run, in the Pause menu, at launch, or during a purchase or restore. Your stars and progress are saved first. If no ad is available, or you are offline, the game simply continues.

Settings, under "Ads & Privacy", shows your current choices. There you can change your age range, switch ads off or on, open Apple's tracking setting, buy Remove Ads, or use Report an ad, which prepares an email to us with details of the last ad shown (you choose whether to send it). Switching ads off stops new ad requests at once. Ad software that has already started stays loaded until you close the game, and it is not started at later launches. Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase that turns ads off; on your other devices, Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account brings it back. It is never needed just to avoid ads. Keeping your progress safe

  • Progress saves automatically on your device. There is no account or iCloud sync, so keep a backup before replacing your device or deleting the app.
  • Back up: open Settings, then "Progress & backups", and tap "Copy a backup". Paste the whole text into Notes, Mail or Files.
  • Merge: copy the whole backup text, then tap "Merge a backup from the clipboard". Merging keeps completed courses, combines banked stars and keeps better times. It never removes progress you have, and it keeps this device's finishes and settings. Repeating the same merge adds nothing twice.
  • "That text isn't a Mint Marble backup" means the clipboard holds something else: copy the whole backup and merge again. "The save is incomplete or damaged" means part of the backup is missing or was changed: copy the complete original again, without editing it.
  • If the game says "Progress is only in this session", copy a backup before leaving the app and email us the exact message. Don't delete the app to fix a problem before your progress is backed up.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check Sound effects in Settings, the ring/silent switch and the volume.
  • The marble keeps rolling after switching apps: the press is released when the app loses focus; tap "Keep playing" and touch the screen again.
  • A purchase is missing: in Settings, under "Purchases", use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that bought it (see above).
  • No ads appear although you allowed them: ads need "18 or older", ads allowed, and tracking allowed in iOS (Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking). Even then an ad appears only occasionally, and only when one is available. Nothing in the game depends on ads.

Reporting a problem

Please include the version shown in Settings, then About; your device model and iOS version; the course number or screen; the steps that lead to the problem; and the exact message, if any. A short screen recording helps with touch or display problems. Send a backup only if you choose to share your progress details. Please don't send passwords.
